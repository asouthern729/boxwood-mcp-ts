# Line-of-Business Checklist Bank

Source: Boxwood's IIABA-derived agency checklists (BOP, Commercial Property, Workers' Compensation,
Professional Liability, Commercial Umbrella/Excess, Contractors) plus the general Commercial Lines
Coverage Checklist. Use this file to decide which items to check for a given account and to recognize
the standard endorsement/coverage names an underwriter or CSR would expect to see addressed.

Pick the checklist(s) that match the account's operations — most commercial accounts will draw from
2-4 of these, not all of them. A residential contractor pulls from Contractors + Commercial Property +
Workers' Comp + Commercial Umbrella, for example. A professional services firm pulls from Professional
Liability + BOP. Always check GL/Property/WC/Umbrella basics regardless of niche, then layer in the
niche-specific checklist(s).

---

## 1. BOP (Businessowners Policy) — small/mid accounts written on a package BOP form

**Underwriting**: Named insured & entity type confirmed against Secretary of State; sister/subsidiary
operations; year building built; operation description; tenant vs. owner (if tenant, is there a lease
requirement to insure the building — attach Additional Insured–Building Owner endorsement); building
construction; gross revenue vs. gross sales; total payroll; employee count; protective safeguards
(sprinkler/alarm); exposures from other operations and from nature (flood/wind/earthquake zone);
insurance-to-value; updates to major systems (heating/plumbing/roofing/wiring); stock/contents value
fluctuation; website checked for undisclosed operations.

**Property options to check**: Additional Covered Property (foundations/bulkheads/piers — BOP excludes
by default), Agreed Value, Backup of Sewer & Drain, Bailee (property of others in care/custody/control),
Boiler & Machinery, Business Income (Actual Loss Sustained for 12 months — is that enough? consider
extending Period of Indemnity), Landlord Loss Payee, Off-Premises Coverage for Vehicles/Mobile Equipment,
Computer Fraud & Funds Transfer Fraud, Crime, Debris Removal limit increase, Deductibles (property vs.
wind/hail), Dependent Properties ($5,000 BOP default — consider increasing; suppliers/buyers/
manufacturing locations/leader locations), Earthquake, Electronic Commerce endorsement, EDP Equipment,
Employee Dishonesty, Fences, Fine Arts, Flood (endorsement or NFIP), Food Contamination, Fungi limited
coverage, Green Upgrades, Identity Fraud, Improvements & Betterments, Increase in Building Cost Following
Disaster, Information Security Protection (data restoration, PR expenses, breach expenses, extortion,
business income, website liability), Insurance-to-Value endorsement (removes coinsurance), Mortgagee,
Ordinance or Law (recommend for any building 5+ years old; note the BOP does NOT cover increased period
of restoration unless the endorsement option is chosen), Payroll Limitation (unendorsed BOP caps
"ordinary employee" payroll coverage at 60 days), Perishable Property/Spoilage, Property in Transit,
Property of Others, Protective Safeguards endorsement (try to have removed if it's just an underwriter
credit-recapture device), Signs, Sprinkler Leakage–Earthquake, Theft of Client Property, Tools &
Equipment, Utility Service (Direct Damage and Time Element), Vacancy Provisions, Water Backup/Sump Pump.

**Liability options**: Additional Insureds (18 available BOP AI endorsements), Broadened Coverage for
Damage to Premises Rented to You, Electronic Data/Internet Liability, Employee Benefits Liability
(claims-made by endorsement).

## 2. Commercial Property (standalone/monoline or package property)

**Underwriting**: Prior dec page (don't rely on it unless you wrote/confirmed that prior term);
ACORD 125 & 140 signed; all locations scheduled (ask specifically about new locations, fences,
outbuildings); year built (ties to Ordinance or Law); tenant vs. owner + tax records confirming building
ownership; if tenant, lease requirement to insure the building (attach CP 12 19 Additional Insured —
Building Owner); construction type per building; updates to major systems; protective safeguards in good
working order (watch for carrier attaching CP 04 11 Protective Safeguards endorsement — try to remove);
flood/earthquake zone; insurance-to-value.

**Coverage items**: Additional Covered Property (CP 14 10/14 15 — foundations/bulkheads/piers excluded
by default), Agreed Value (with annual value updates), Antennas, Backup of Sewer & Drain, Bailee's
(care/custody/control), Boiler & Machinery, Builders' Risk (any construction projects contemplated?),
Business Income & Extra Expense, Cargo, Cause of Loss form used (Basic/Broad/Special), Crime, Deductible
matches expiring, Difference in Conditions, Earthquake, EDP Equipment, Equipment Breakdown, Fences,
Finished Goods (selling price/cost), Flood, Glass (owner/tenant), Green Upgrades, Improvements &
Betterments, Inland Marine, Installation, Laptops, Leasehold Interest (when tenant has a favorable lease),
Limits — blanket vs. scheduled (explain the margin clause if present), Monthly Reporting, Mortgagee,
Ordinance or Law (CP 04 05 — 5+ year old buildings), Perishable Property (CP 04 40), Property in Transit,
Property of Others, Signs, Sprinkler Leakage, Tools (owner/employee), Trip Transit, Utility Service Direct
Damage, Vacancy Provisions, Valuation (ACV/Functional/Market/Replacement Cost).

## 3. Workers' Compensation

**Underwriting**: Entity type drives "employee" status (corporate officers = employees; sole
proprietors/partners generally aren't; LLC members vary by state statute); all office/other locations
scheduled; any employees/officers/members excluded from coverage (list names/duties — explain the health
insurance/coverage-gap implications); members/managers/sole props/partners opting IN to coverage;
employees living or working outside the state of domicile (list states — may need to be added as 3.A. or
3.C. states); home-based employees (ergonomic compliance); employees who regularly travel out of state;
employer-furnished group transportation; employees running errands or in employer-sponsored recreational
activities in their own car (compensability exposure); chemical/x-ray/radiation exposure; Hazard
Communication Plan (OSHA 29 CFR 1910-1200) and MSDS on file even for office-only exposures; PPE
provided/inspected/employees trained; independent contractors/subcontractors used — current COIs
required of all of them (uninsured IC/SC employees can become the de jure responsibility of the upper-tier
contractor depending on state law); sample contracts reviewed (both hold-harmless-given and
risk-transferred); states of ongoing/temporary operations all listed under 3.A.; **monopolistic states
(ND, OH, WA, WY) require state-fund WC plus a separate stand-alone Employers' Liability/stop-gap policy**
since the private carrier's Part One can't respond there; Second Injury Fund states — pre-existing
conditions; temporary labor/subcontractors working in states not yet listed as 3.A.; plans to expand into
new states; CG 21 39 exclusion attached to the CGL (removes WC-alternative coverage there); employees
traveling outside the US; employees on watercraft/navigable waters (USL&HW "status"/"situs" tests) or
maritime exposure; employees on military bases; leased employees (Alternate Employer Endorsement) or PEO
co-employment (endorsements needed on both the direct employer's and the PEO's policy); "borrowed"
workers; combinable operations under common ownership (past 5 years); payroll splits kept separate when
an employee does more than one job (interchange-of-labor rule); employees exempt from WC by statute (attach
Voluntary Compensation endorsement if the employer wants to cover them anyway); **Employers' Liability
limits sufficient to satisfy the umbrella/excess policy's underlying requirement**; prior audit reviewed;
experience mod calculation reviewed.

## 4. Professional Liability

**Underwriting**: Year business launched; entity name/type per Secretary of State; any preceding
entity (is it covered?); years of experience of current ownership; description of operations; professional
association/board memberships (board membership may need its own endorsement or separate D&O-style
coverage); **polling letters requested of all owners/officers/managers** (confirms no unreported prior
incidents that could jeopardize coverage); written contracts used with all clients (even pro bono?).

**Policy mechanics**: Admitted vs. non-admitted carrier; per-occurrence/aggregate limits and whether
there's a per-claim aggregate; deductible/SIR, including whether it's an aggregate deductible; coverage
territory; is the application part of the policy (makes it a warranty, not just a representation);
**claims-made vs. occurrence**, and if claims-made: current retroactive date (confirm concurrency), prior/
pending-claim or continuity date, reporting requirements ("as soon as practicable" language); if moving
from claims-made to occurrence or advancing the retro date, is **tail coverage** available and needed;
Extended Reporting Period terms — restricted or unrestricted, conditioned on cancellation/non-renewal by
either party, length, and pricing for supplemental ERP options; duty to defend vs. duty to reimburse (and
if reimburse, obligation to "keep current" on material changes); Consent to Settle / "Hammer Clause" —
is there a soft-hammer option; who qualifies as an Insured (are independent contractors covered — is a
contract required of them); defense inside limits, outside limits, or subject to a separate limit (and
what happens to future defense costs once a separate defense limit is exhausted); **definition of
"Professional Services"** — broad enough for current operations, and are there exclusions for services
the insured performed in the past but no longer offers; definition of "Wrongful Act"; definition of
"Claim" (does it require receipt by the insured); choice of defense counsel and any restrictions;
mandatory arbitration (claimant vs. insured and/or insured vs. insurer).

## 5. Commercial Umbrella / Excess

**Underwriting**: Entity name/type confirmed vs. Secretary of State and vs. all underlying policies;
former entities (are they covered?); which underlying policies the umbrella sits above (CGL, Business
Auto, Employers' Liability, other); **underlying limit requirements met for each** (frequent E&O
exposure) — Per Occurrence/Aggregate for CGL, BAP limit, Employers' Liability Each Accident/Disease
Policy Limit/Disease Per Employee; underlying carriers identified; any underlying coverage on a
claims-made basis, and if so do retro dates match.

**Umbrella/excess terms**: Per Occurrence/Aggregate limits — optional higher limits presented and
rejection documented if declined ("Higher Limits Available" disclosure is a common E&O gap);
deductible/SIR; **policy effective dates concurrent with underlying**; coverage territory same/narrower/
wider than underlying; when Duty to Defend is triggered (generally right-to-defend, not duty, until
underlying limits exhaust); defense costs inside or outside limits; **drop-down protection** when
underlying aggregate exhausts; underlying CGL Additional Insureds carried through to the umbrella;
type of umbrella/excess form — true Umbrella (excess + fills gaps, subject to SIR) vs. Following Form
Excess (excess only, no broader coverage) vs. Self-Contained (may have exclusions not in the underlying
forms); **UM/UIM coverage offered and rejection documented if declined** (common E&O exposure);
broadening endorsements on the underlying also attached to the umbrella; exclusionary/limiting
endorsements on the umbrella not present underlying (narrows coverage) or on the underlying not present
on the umbrella (widens it — note the difference); Non-Contributory/Order of Response endorsement when a
contract requires vertical exhaustion; if multiple layers, confirm each layer's limits/carrier and
whether it's as broad as the layer below it.

## 6. Contractors

**Underwriting**: Named insured confirmed vs. Secretary of State; entity type and any changes in the
last 5 years; year business started; years of experience of current owners; bonding carrier(s);
description of operations; licensing authority; average/largest project values (past 12 months and past
5 years); average number of projects per year and simultaneous projects; employee count; **work
subcontracted to others** — percentage, whether it's work the contractor could do itself vs. work it
lacks skill/manpower for, who manages subcontracted work, whether all subs work under written contract,
and **whether subcontractor certificates of insurance are actually reviewed** (this is the single most
common E&O gap for a GC/builder).

**Contractor's Inland Marine — Equipment/Machinery**: description and total value; schedule anything
over $5,000; blanket vs. scheduled basis; replacement cost valuation; covered causes of loss; deductible;
equipment leased/borrowed from others (frequency, length, with/without operator, formal agreement always
used, ACV and replacement cost value) and equipment leased/loaned to others (same questions); written
maintenance/inspection/replacement schedule.

**Contractor's Tools**: total value; schedule anything over $5,000; blanket vs. scheduled; replacement
cost; covered causes of loss; deductible; leased/borrowed tools (same lease-agreement questions as
equipment); coverage for tools owned by employees (total value, ACV vs. replacement cost).

**Installation Floater**: needed whenever property at a construction site — owned by or the
responsibility of the insured — will become part of the finished structure (this is NOT covered under
Builders' Risk); coverage limit, valuation, covered causes of loss, and whether coverage is needed while
in transit or in temporary storage.

**Builders' Risk**: Reporting Form vs. "One-Shot" Form; covered causes of loss, and specifically whether
Flood and Earthquake are included; Water Intrusion coverage (distinct from flood — intrusion during
construction from contractor oversight/negligence, not physical damage); valuation method (ACV standard,
Replacement Cost optional); **who is insured** — General Contractor, Principal/Owner, Subcontractors
(insurable interest must attach), Mortgagee; **covered property** — property being constructed, materials
and supplies that will become part of the structure (this takes the place of a separate Installation
Floater), foundations, temporary structures, scaffolding (cost to re-erect covered?), construction forms,
fencing.

## 7. General Commercial Lines Coverage Checklist (all-lines crosswalk / output template)

This is the umbrella structure the finished output document follows, spanning every line. Use it to make
sure nothing falls between the cracks of the niche checklists above, and as the section order for the
deliverable.

**Property**: Blanket vs. scheduled buildings (Basic/Broad/Special form each); Causes exclusions;
Additional Covered Property (14 10), Additional Building Property (14 15), Additional Property Not
Covered (14 20); Replacement Cost / Agreed Value options; Inflation Guard; Multiple Deductible (03 20);
Debris Removal limit (04 15); Newly Acquired property limit (04 25); Functional Building Valuation
(04 39); Ordinance or Law (04 05); Condominium forms; Legal Liability Coverage (00 40); Builders' Risk
(00 20); Outdoor Signs (14 40); Glass (00 16); Deductible; Personal Property (scheduled/blanket, property
of others, leased property 14 60); Separation of Coverage (19 10); Market Value–Stock (99 31); Mfg.
Consequential Loss/Selling Price (99 02/99 30); Functional Valuation (04 39); Reporting Form (13 10);
Peak Season (12 30); Off-Premises Power Failure (04 17, including transmission lines); Spoilage (04 40).

**Time Element**: Business Income & Extra Expense / Business Income w/o Extra Expense / Extra Expense
alone; Rental Value; Dependent Property; Tuition Fees; Leasehold Interest; Maximum Period of Indemnity;
Monthly Limitation; Agreed Value option; Extended Period of Indemnity; Ordinary Payroll (15 10); Power/
Heat/Refrigeration Deduction (15 11); Electronic Media (15 19); Ordinance or Law (15 31); Off-Premises
Services (15 45).

**General Liability**: Occurrence/General Aggregate/Products Aggregate/Fire Damage limits; Per Project
or Per Premises Aggregate (25 03/25 04); Separate project/premises limit (25 01); Employment Practices
Exclusion (21 47); Product/XCU/Professional exclusions; Additional Insured schedule; Liquor Liability
(24 08); Products Redefined (24 07); Personal Injury Contractual (22 74); Waiver of Subrogation (24 04);
Employee Benefits Liability.

**Inland Marine**: Signs (00 28); Valuable Papers (00 67); Accounts Receivable (00 66); Cameras/Musical
Instruments (00 21); Physicians & Surgeons Equipment (00 26); Fine Arts; Equipment Floater; Installation
Floater; Builders' Risk; Bailee Liability; Motor Truck Cargo; Installment Sales; Patterns/Dies/Molds;
Mail; Replacement Cost; Additionally Covered Property; Reporting.

**Crime**: Employee Dishonesty (A); Forgery/Alteration (B); Money & Securities Inside/Outside (C);
Robbery/Safe Burglary (D); Premises Burglary (E); Premises Theft (H); Robbery-Safe Burglary Form Q (I).

**Boiler & Machinery**: Object Form (including/excluding production); Small Business B&M / Broad Form;
Spoilage; Business Interruption (Actual Loss Sustained vs. Valued Forms); Extra Expense; Explosion
Elimination; Deductible.

**Business Auto**: Liability (owned vs. Hired/Non-Owned only); Med Pay/PIP; UM/UIM; Comp/Collision
deductibles and Specified Causes; Individual Named Insured (99 17); Additional Insured – Lessor (20 01);
Employees as Insureds (99 33); Drive Other Car (99 10); Garagekeepers (99 37); Hired Car Physical Damage;
Rental Reimbursement (99 23A); Towing & Labor; state filings (e.g., TxDOT); Mexico Coverage Limited
(04 07).

**Workers' Compensation**: Employers' Liability; Other States schedule; Monopolistic State Operations /
Stopgap; Foreign Operations; USL&HW; Maritime; Voluntary Compensation; Sole Proprietor/Officer/Partner
election; Waiver of Subrogation; Alternate Employer; Notice of Material Change endorsement.

**Electronic Equipment Protection**: Hardware/Media; Business Interruption; Extra Expenses; Mechanical
Breakdown; Electrical Injury; Automatic Extinguisher; Interruption of Power Off-Premises.

**Miscellaneous**: Umbrella Liability; Professional Liability; Flood; Earthquake; Difference in
Conditions; Directors & Officers Liability; Watercraft; Aircraft; Fiduciary Liability (incl. Employee
Benefit Mgmt.); Employment Practices Liability; Environmental Impairment Liability; Foreign Products/
Operations; Discontinued Products/Operations; Bonds; Life; Health; Disability Income.
