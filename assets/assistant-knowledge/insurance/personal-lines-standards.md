# Personal Lines Coverage Standards (Boxwood Insurance Group)

Source: Boxwood's internal "Personal Lines Coverage Standards" document. These are Boxwood's own
*minimum-to-recommend* quoting standards for Auto and Homeowners — not a checklist of what a policy
theoretically could carry, but what every Boxwood producer/CSR is expected to have offered and, where
declined, documented. That distinction matters for how gaps get classified (see "How to score personal
lines" at the end of this file).

---

## Auto Insurance Coverage Standards

**Liability Coverage**
- Quote minimum **$250,000 / $500,000 Bodily Injury**
- Quote minimum **$100,000 Property Damage**
- Discuss CSL options when available (e.g. Hanover $500k CSL is cheaper than split limits for a
  comparable client)
- Avoid lower limits unless the client specifically requests them
- **The absolute floor Boxwood will write is 100/300/100** — anything at or below that requires
  documentation that higher limits were advised in writing and/or a signed Coverage Rejection Statement
- Document any coverage waiver where limits are below the $250k/$500k/$100k standard

**Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist (UM/UIM)**
- Match UM/UIM limits to Bodily Injury limits
- Offer minimum **$250,000 / $500,000 / $100,000**
- Never reduce UM/UIM below the BI limit
- Document any UM/UIM reduction or rejection
- **Auto-Owners-specific**: UM/UIM must be $500k/$500k/$500k for the client to have UM coverage on an
  Auto-Owners umbrella — flag this explicitly whenever the carrier is Auto-Owners and an umbrella is also
  on the account

**Medical Payments**
- Offer on every quote
- Minimum **$5,000**
- Recommend **$10,000–$25,000** for more affluent accounts

**Comprehensive & Collision**
- Required for financed or leased vehicles
- Deductible: **$1,000 standard recommendation**
- Always quote full glass coverage on Comprehensive
- **Cincinnati-specific**: if the policy is not written on a Capstone auto form, the Personal Auto Plus
  endorsement must be added

**Rental Reimbursement**
- Offer on all policies
- Minimum **$50/day**

**Towing & Roadside Assistance**
- Always quote
- Confirm mileage allowance is increased on carriers that default to only 15 miles

**Loan/Lease Gap Coverage**
- Add to newly-added financed vehicles
- Most carriers restrict this to brand-new or few-years-old vehicles only — the carrier system should
  flag if it isn't allowed for a given vehicle; don't force it if it's genuinely unavailable

**Replacement Cost / New-or-Newer Car Replacement**
- Always add when available on a brand-new vehicle (current or prior model year)
- **Hanover-specific**: add "New and Newer Car Replacement Guard"; when a new-car change request is
  emailed to the CSC, remind them to add this coverage on the new vehicle

## Homeowners Insurance Coverage Standards

**Dwelling Coverage (Coverage A)**
- Insure to **100% replacement cost**
- Minimum **$275/sq ft**; higher-value homes need **$350–$450/sq ft**
- For condos, the per-sq-ft figure depends on location — flag for producer input (referenced internally
  as "ask Paige or Kimbra") rather than guessing a number
- Run the carrier's cost estimator when required
- Confirm updates are reflected: roof, kitchens, baths, additions, finished basements
- Always add **Replacement Cost (extended replacement cost) of at least 50%** where the carrier offers it

**Other Structures (Coverage B)**
- Standard **10% of Coverage A**
- Always ask about detached structures (garages, sheds, fences, pools); if there are multiple, confirm
  the client is comfortable that 10% is enough rather than assuming it is

**Personal Property (Coverage C)**
- Standard **50–70% of Coverage A**
- Always quote **replacement cost**, never ACV
- Add **Special Personal Property** (open-perils) where the carrier offers it as an option
- Review high-value items / off-premises exposure; quote **Personal Articles** floater for jewelry, fine
  art, collectibles

**Loss of Use (Coverage D)**
- Minimum **20% of Coverage A**; recommend **30–40%** when available
- Discuss extended rebuild timelines and local housing costs
- Not required on a secondary residence if the client doesn't want it — that's an acceptable client
  choice, not a gap, on a second home specifically

**Personal Liability**
- Minimum **$500,000**; recommend **$1,000,000** for affluent clients
- Review exposures: pools, trampolines, dogs

**Medical Payments to Others**
- Minimum **$5,000**; recommend **$10,000**

**Deductibles**
- Standard **$2,500 All Other Perils (AOP)**
- **$5,000 AOP** for homes with a **$1,000,000+** dwelling value
- Review every deductible type separately: All Other Perils, Wind/Hail (flat or %), Named Storm/Hurricane
  where applicable
- Document client approval whenever a deductible is set above standard

**Water Damage / Water Back-Up**
- Water Back-Up endorsement is **required** on every policy — scale the limit to dwelling value:
  - Dwelling < $300,000 → quote $25,000
  - Dwelling $300,001–$500,000 → quote $50,000
  - Dwelling $500,001–$999,999 → quote $100,000
  - Dwelling $1M+ → quote up to the policy limit / carrier maximum available

**Mold Coverage**
- Increased Mold Coverage endorsement required on every policy, minimum **$25,000**
- If dwelling > $500,000, increase to **$50,000 or 10% of dwelling coverage** (whichever the carrier's
  ceiling allows — some carriers cap at $50,000 regardless)

**Sinkhole Coverage**
- Quote on every policy where the carrier offers it as an endorsement/optional coverage
- Note: Cincinnati includes sinkhole in its base policy form (nothing to add)

**Service Line Coverage**
- Offer on every policy (water, sewer, electric, gas, internet lines) — minimum **$10,000** limit

**Equipment Breakdown**
- Offer on every policy

**Cyber & Identity Protection**
- Offer Cyber coverage on every quote
- If Cyber isn't available from that carrier, offer Identity Theft/ID Fraud protection instead

**Flood**
- Offer a flood insurance quote on every property **regardless of flood zone**

**Earthquake**
- Not automatically quoted — available through most carriers, offer if the client wants it (absence here
  is not itself a gap unless the client has expressed interest or the property has known seismic exposure)

## Carrier-Specific Homeowners Endorsement Rules

**Cincinnati**
- Executive form — dwelling coverage up to $749,000: includes Homeowners Plus, Limited Water Damage
- Executive Select form — dwelling $750,000–$1,499,999: Enhanced Replacement Cost on Coverage A (either
  100% or Guaranteed Replacement Cost), Limited Water Damage
- Executive Capstone form — dwelling $1,500,000+: **do not add a Water Damage Limitation/Exclusion** —
  if one is present on a Capstone-tier policy, that's a real finding (the client is getting less water
  damage coverage than the tier they're paying for), not just a documentation gap
- Umbrella: a $5M umbrella increases the homeowners discount; if the client carries a $3M umbrella,
  quote $5M at renewal — the added home discount usually exceeds the incremental umbrella cost. Flag this
  as a **savings opportunity**, not a coverage gap, when a Cincinnati home + sub-$5M umbrella combination
  is found.
- Sinkhole is bundled into Cincinnati's policy form already — never flag it as missing on a Cincinnati
  policy.

**Hanover**
- Dwelling coverage up to $749,999 → Select Premium Policy Form
- Dwelling coverage $750,000+ → Prestige Policy Form
- New and Newer Car Replacement Guard is the Hanover-specific auto endorsement name (see Auto section
  above)

**Auto-Owners**
- UM/UIM must be carried at $500k/$500k/$500k for the client to retain UM coverage on an Auto-Owners
  umbrella (see Auto section above) — always check this combination explicitly when the carrier is
  Auto-Owners and an umbrella is on file.

## How to score personal lines against these standards

Unlike the commercial-lines checklists, these are **quoting minimums Boxwood has already committed to
offering** — so scoring works slightly differently:

- **IN FORCE** — the coverage/limit on file meets or exceeds the standard above.
- **GAP** — the coverage is either absent entirely, or present but **below** the stated minimum/standard
  (e.g. BI limits at 100/300/100 with no rejection statement on file, or a Water Back-Up limit below the
  dwelling-value-scaled minimum). Per Boxwood policy, anything scored GAP here needs the client advised in
  writing and/or a signed Coverage Rejection Statement before it can be treated as an accepted choice — say
  this explicitly in the Notes column, don't just say "confirm with client."
- **CONFIRM** — can't tell from the data pulled whether the standard is met (e.g. deductible-approval
  documentation isn't visible in AMS360, or a carrier-specific per-sq-ft dwelling figure needs producer
  judgment).
- **N/A** — a standard that's explicitly conditional and the condition doesn't apply (e.g. Loss of Use on
  a primary residence is never N/A, but on a documented secondary/seasonal residence it can be).
- **EXCL** — a coverage the carrier's form affirmatively excludes or limits (e.g. a Water Damage
  Limitation present on a Cincinnati Capstone-tier policy, which shouldn't be there).
