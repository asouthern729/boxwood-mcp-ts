Boxwood MCP Tools & Skills

Skill

Domain knowledge invoked in Claude.ai by typing the /skill-name.

MCP Tool

Runs behind the scenes to fetch data — not called directly in Claude.ai.

Customers

/customerscustomer_lookup

Find a customer — contact info, policies, claims, certificates, service team — browsable across the whole book.

The starting point for any question about a specific customer.

Policies

/policiespolicy_query

Find a policy — transactions, coverages, lines of business, coverage-line pricing, vehicles, workers' comp, forms, applicant detail, renewal-chain history.

For any policy-specific question — coverage detail, an endorsement, or how a term compares to its renewal.

/renewalsupcoming_renewals

What's renewing soon and still needs outreach — premium totals, renewal progress, with marketing shells and already-renewed terms filtered out.

Use for renewal outreach planning, check what renewals are coming in the future up to 1-year out.

Claims

/claimsclaim_lookup

Find a claim — payments, injuries, damage, adjuster notes, including how much has actually been paid out.

Use when a customer or adjuster question needs claim-level detail.

Billing

/invoicesinvoice_lookup

Find an invoice — billing detail, cancellations, related activity — not outstanding balances, which AMS360 doesn't track here.

Helpful for tracing billing history or a cancellation, not for checking what's currently outstanding.

Certificates

/certificatescertificate_lookup

Find a certificate of insurance — holder, coverage, reissue history, and what a COI actually does or doesn't cover.

Use to check current certificate holders on an account, or issue/reissue counts for a customer.

Staff

employee_lookup

Find a staff member — their full book of customers, policies, claims.

Use when reviewing one producer's or CSR's full book of business.

Agency-wide

book_summary

Current book broken out by producer, CSR, carrier, or line — premium, counts, claims paid, agency-wide by default or scoped to one segment.

Use for agency-wide totals or a full breakdown by producer, CSR, carrier, or line.

/activityactivity_feed

What's changed lately — new policies, endorsements, claims, invoices, unified into one audit-trail timeline.

Good for catching up after time away, or spot-checking recent activity on an account.

Downloads

/downloadsdownload_report

Morning download review — overnight carrier policy transactions and claims, normalized into action items and grouped by rep, rebuilt from synced data instead of AMS360's raw download log.

Run this first thing each morning to see what came down overnight.

/downloadsdownload_report_workbook

Stage 2 of the download review — the finished .xlsx built from download_report, with flagged rows and accuracy verdicts, one tab per rep.

Use once the review's done and it's time to hand reps their action list.
