Boxwood MCP Tools & Skills

/skill

Domain knowledge invoked in Claude.ai by typing the /skill-name.

mcp_tool

Runs behind the scenes to fetch data — not called directly in Claude.ai.

Customers

/customerscustomer_lookup

Find a customer — contact info, policies, claims, certificates, service team — browsable across the whole book.

The starting point for any question about a specific customer.

Policies

/policiespolicy_query

Find a policy — transactions, coverages, lines of business, coverage-line pricing, vehicles, workers' comp, forms, applicant detail, renewal-chain history.

For any policy-specific question — coverage detail, an endorsement, or how a term compares to its renewal.

/renewalsupcoming_renewals

What's renewing soon — premium totals, renewal progress, with marketing shells always filtered out.

Use for renewal outreach planning, check what renewals are coming in the future up to 1-year out. By default also filters out already-renewed terms (a "needs outreach" pipeline view) — pass include_already_renewed for a calendar/exposure view that includes those too.

/renewalsrisk_profile

Branded Pre‑Renewal Review .docx for one or more commercial policies — named insureds, locations, property (one row per address, Building/BPP limits split), GL and workers' comp exposure, equipment, vehicles, and drivers, pulled live from AMS360.

Returns a download link only, no email. Matching several current policies for the same customer (optionally scoped to a renewal window, e.g. the next 90 days) combines them into one document. Exposures only, no premiums or coverages. Commercial lines only.

renewal_premium_summary

Commercial Renewal Premium Summary .xlsx for an account — one row per policy renewing in a given window (premium, carrier, lines of business bundled on it), plus a second table of the account's other current policies for later quote notes.

Returns a download link for the CSR to review — no email, that's been removed entirely. Reports premium per policy, not split by line of business — AMS360 bills most multi-line Package policies as one blended rate, so a per-LOB split would be unreliable exactly where it'd matter most. Commercial lines only, always account-scoped.

Claims

/claimsclaim_lookup

Find a claim — payments, injuries, damage, adjuster notes, including how much has actually been paid out.

Use when a customer or adjuster question needs claim-level detail.

Billing

/invoicesinvoice_lookup

Find an invoice — billing detail, cancellations, related activity — not outstanding balances, which AMS360 doesn't track here.

Helpful for tracing billing history or a cancellation, not for checking what's currently outstanding.

Certificates

/certificatescertificate_lookup

Find a certificate of insurance — holder, coverage, reissue history, and what a COI actually does or doesn't cover.

Use to check current certificate holders on an account, or issue/reissue counts for a customer.

Staff

employee_lookup

Find a staff member — their full book of customers, policies, claims, and invoices.

Use when reviewing one producer's or CSR's full book of business.

Agency-wide

book_summary

Current book broken out by producer, CSR, carrier, or line — premium, counts, claims paid, agency-wide by default or scoped to one segment.

Use for agency-wide totals or a full breakdown by producer, CSR, carrier, or line.

/activityactivity_feed

What's changed lately — new policies, endorsements, claims, invoices, unified into one audit-trail timeline.

Good for catching up after time away, or spot-checking recent activity on an account.

Downloads

/downloadsdownload_report

Morning download review — overnight carrier policy transactions and claims, normalized into action items and grouped by rep, rebuilt from synced data instead of AMS360's raw download log.

Run this first thing each morning to see what came down overnight.

/downloadsdownload_report_workbook

Stage 2 of the download review — the finished .xlsx built from download_report, with flagged rows and accuracy verdicts, one tab per rep.

Use once the review's done and it's time to hand reps their action list.

Roadmap

board_summary

View the shared tool roadmap — items by category/status/name, each with its full comment thread between Andrew and the client.

Use to catch up on the roadmap before posting a comment or changing a status.

board_update_item

Update a roadmap item — name, status, priority level, or notes, by id or name match.

Use after finishing work on something, or to reprioritize.

board_comment

Post a comment on a roadmap item — visible to both Andrew and the client on the page, attributed to Claude.

Use to leave a note on the roadmap the same way Andrew or the client would.

board_create_item

Add a new tool idea to the roadmap — category, name, and any of priority/status/notes/etc. up front.

Use when a new idea comes up in conversation so it doesn't get lost.

board_delete_item

Permanently delete a roadmap item and its comments — prompts for confirmation before deleting anything, and won't proceed without it.

Destructive and can't be undone — use to remove an idea that's no longer relevant.

board_edit_comment

Rewrite one of Claude's own roadmap comments — refuses on a comment posted by Andrew or the client.

Use to shorten or refine an earlier comment once later replies settle what actually mattered.

board_delete_comment

Delete one of Claude's own roadmap comments — refuses on a comment posted by Andrew or the client; no confirmation prompt needed since it never touches a human's comment.

Use to remove a comment that's now redundant or superseded.
